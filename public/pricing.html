FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a setup fee?
No. RentPayEZ is free to set up. The Starter plan is completely free forever. Pro and Business plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
What payment methods are accepted for rent?
We accept ACH bank transfers, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Your landlord or property manager may restrict certain methods.
How does credit reporting work?
RentPayEZ reports your on-time rent payments to Equifax and TransUnion automatically each month at no extra cost on Pro and Business plans. You'll see your score reflected within 30–60 days.
Is my data and payment information secure?
Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all transactions and are PCI-DSS compliant. We never store your full card or bank details on our servers.
Do landlords pay to use RentPayEZ?
Landlords and property managers use the Business plan. Tenants can use the Starter plan for free, making it easy to get your whole portfolio onboarded at no cost to your tenants.

Still have questions?

Our team is here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
